About

I build things that fly — and I'm fascinated by the physics underneath every one of them.

I'm a B.Tech Aerospace Engineering student at IIT Kharagpur, driven by a simple curiosity: how do we make flight lighter, smarter, and more autonomous? That question pulls me across disciplines — from designing competition UAVs and optimizing them against tight dynamic constraints, to teaching neural networks the laws of aerodynamics.

I currently work as a research intern across two labs at IIT KGP — running laser-Doppler experiments on bluff-body wake flows, and building Physics-Informed Neural Networks for trajectory and launch-condition prediction. I like problems where a clean equation and a clever model meet a hard real-world constraint.

PROJECT 02 · R&D INTERNSHIP

PINNs for Trajectory & Launch-Condition Prediction

Built and validated a Physics-Informed Neural Network pipeline for projectile dynamics across five models, benchmarked against analytical and RK4 ground truth. Developed a generalised parametric PINN that learns the full launch-parameter (u, θ) space in a single network — keeping 100% of configurations under 5% error where a single-config baseline collapses to ~55%. Applied inverse PINN parametrization to recover hidden launch conditions and drag from observed motion. Manuscript in preparation.
